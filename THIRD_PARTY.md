# Third-party code

The repair engine is in `kryoto-desktop/crates/kryoto-repair`. Its `crack.rs`,
`settings.rs`, `tools.rs`, `peversion.rs` and `child_guard.rs` came from Kryoto
Forge. The shared crate uses the MIT license.

The app downloads emulator releases from their upstream projects. Each
emulator has its own license. Emulator DLLs are not included in this repository.
